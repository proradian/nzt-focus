## Features

- **Focus blocks and breaks.** 60 minutes of work, then a 10–15 minute break; every third break is long, with micro-breaks every 20 minutes.
- **Honest time tracking.** When a block ends, the timer stops. Sleep and shutdown do not count as work.
- **Distraction blocking.** During a block, Focus minimizes YouTube, VK, Instagram, Telegram Web and Yandex Music, and covers the window if you bring it back. You can switch off built-in sites and add your own by domain. In browsers the site is detected by the tab's address.
- **Entry music.** By default a block starts with calm instrumental music from Tabletop Audio: it plays for 2 minutes, fades out evenly and is silent by minute 5. You can also choose silence or music for the whole block.
- **Living background** that slowly flows across the window, plus daily history and a weekly chart.

## Based on research

See the [list of studies behind each feature](docs/research/SOURCES.md) (in Russian, with links to the papers). The reports in [`docs/research`](docs/research) cover focus length, breaks and micro-breaks, and background sound. The short version: music without lyrics lifts mood and makes the start more pleasant, but complex thinking goes better in silence.

- **Untampered file.** SHA-256 of `NZT-Focus-setup.exe` version 1.0.0:
  `838f497f983309915610990d462880e6df65c0db66e230b1f022a78d187e1f99`
  Check in PowerShell: `Get-FileHash .\NZT-Focus-setup.exe` — the values must match.
- **What it does on your computer.** Stores data only locally in `%APPDATA%\app.focus.timer\`. During a block it reads the title and address of the active browser window to minimize distracting sites. Optionally closes the Yandex Music app. Its only network access is tabletopaudio.com for music.
- **Your own timer instead.** Really worried about viruses? Let an AI write a timer like this for you — [prompt at the end](#prompt-build-your-own-timer).

## Build

Only needed if you want to check the code or change the app; to use it, the Download button above is enough.

Requires [Node.js](https://nodejs.org), [Rust](https://rustup.rs) and WebView2 (included in Windows 10 and 11).

```powershell
cd app
npm install
npx tauri build
```

The installer is created in `app/src-tauri/target/release/bundle/nsis/`.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your computer in `%APPDATA%\app.focus.timer\`. Focus sends nothing anywhere; it only loads tabletopaudio.com when music is playing.

## Prompt: build your own timer

Paste this request into ChatGPT, Claude or another AI. You get a single HTML file that opens in a browser, and all of its code can be read. Keep in mind: a browser page cannot minimize distracting sites and apps, stay on top of other windows or live in the tray, and instead of Tabletop Audio music it plays sound generated by the page itself. The first result will be simpler than NZT Focus and will take a few follow-up requests to polish.

```text
Build a focus timer as a single HTML file: HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file, with no external libraries, fonts or network requests, so it opens with a double click in a browser. Write clear code with short comments so it can be read and checked.

WORK BLOCK
- 60 minutes by default; 25, 45, 60, 90 minutes or a custom length to choose from. An optional "What are you working on?" field; the task stays visible during the block.
- Buttons: pause, break now, finish.
- Count only working time: if the computer slept or the page was closed, that gap is not work (compare real time between ticks and keep the state in localStorage).

SOFT END OF A BLOCK
- When time is up, the timer freezes at the planned time, a soft sound plays and a break is offered.
- If the person finishes a thought and presses a button within 10 minutes, those minutes count toward the block.
- A "5 more minutes" button extends the block, but never beyond 90 minutes per block.
- If there is no response for 15 minutes, the block closes by itself exactly at the planned time.

MICRO-PAUSES
- Every 20 minutes of a block, a gentle 20-second banner: "Look into the distance, relax your shoulders". No pop-ups; it can be dismissed.

BREAK
- 10 minutes; every third break is 25 minutes. Buttons "+5 minutes" and "End break".
- The break screen shows one random screen-free rest idea.
  Short: walk around the room; look out of the window at something far away; stretch; drink water away from the desk; a few slow breaths with a long exhale; look at something green for about forty seconds.
  Long: go outside; eat away from the desk and screens; talk to someone about something other than work; a short walk or light exercise. Between 12:30 and 15:00 add to long breaks: "you can nap for 15–20 minutes, but no longer than 30".
- A line at the bottom: after a break spent on the phone, people solve tasks more slowly than after the same break without it.
- "Break now" in the middle of a block: first a "Where did you stop?" field (one line, optional), then a 5-minute break, then the block continues and the note is shown next to the task.

SOUND
- Three modes on the home screen: "Silence", "Sound to start" (default), "Sound for the whole block".
- The sound is a calm background without melody or words, generated with Web Audio (a soft chord or rain-like noise), no sound files.
- "Sound to start": the background plays for the first 2 minutes of a block, then fades over 3 minutes evenly in decibels — from 0 to −40 dB, i.e. a volume factor of 10^(−40·x/20) with x going from 0 to 1. Silence by minute 5. Do not reduce amplitude linearly: the fade is then inaudible at first and the sound drops off abruptly at the end.
- Start time is counted in working minutes without pauses. A "Stop immersion" button fades the sound out smoothly over 2 seconds; an "Immersion" button restarts the fade-in sequence from the current minute.
- Default volume 30%, slider in settings.

HISTORY
- Save every block to localStorage: date, task, minutes.
- Home screen: blocks and minutes today and a 7-day chart — bars per day labelled with hours.
- After 4 hours of focus in a day, a gentle "that's enough for today" hint: a guideline, not a ban.

LIVING BACKGROUND
- A slow colour flow across the whole window (canvas or CSS gradients): cool colours during focus (blue, teal, violet), warm ones during breaks. Each colour blends into the next in about a minute.
- No flashes, pulsing or sudden animation starts: it is the onset of motion that grabs attention. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- A calm dark interface with a large timer inside a progress ring.

TEXTS
- Do not promise higher productivity and do not use popular myths (goldfish attention span, 90-minute cycles, the 52/17 rule). Talk about less fatigue and a calm rhythm. Keep it short and friendly.

DO NOT
- A browser page cannot minimize other windows and sites, stay on top of all windows or run from the tray. Do not try to do this or fake it.
```
